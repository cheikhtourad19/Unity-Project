This is the Rosetta Stone, a fragment of a granodiorite stele. It bears a decree from one hundred and ninety-six BCE, honouring Ptolemy the Fifth. The same decree appears in three scripts: hieroglyphs, Demotic and Greek. Comparing them helped scholars decipher Egyptian hieroglyphs. Look at the different groups of signs on its surface. This fragment shows how a written text can open a window onto a civilisation.
