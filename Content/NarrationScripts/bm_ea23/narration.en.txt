This granite sarcophagus belonged to Hapmen, a high official in ancient Egypt. Its walls carry texts and figures of funerary deities. Inside, the goddess Nut appears on the bottom of the chest. Notice the reliefs and how they surround the space intended for the deceased. The sarcophagus was later reused for ritual washing in the Ibn Tulun Mosque in Cairo. It therefore preserves traces of different uses across its long history.
