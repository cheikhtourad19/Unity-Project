This statue represents the god Amun as a reclining ram. Between its front legs stands a small figure of King Taharqa, sheltered by the god. The work comes from the temple of Amun at Kawa, in present-day Sudan. Notice the contrast between the large animal and the king, then look at the inscriptions on the base. This composition expresses a connection between royal power and divine protection in the history of Nubia and Egypt.
